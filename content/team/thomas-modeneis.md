I help companies design, build, and run the backend systems that sit on the critical path of real-time requests: matching engines, market-data and price streams, real-time bidding, search, and data APIs.

I have 22 years of software engineering behind me, in roles from founder and CTO to architect and hands-on engineer. I co-founded Vosbor Exchange and built its trading backend from zero, was Chief Architect of Hydration (formerly HydraDX) on Polkadot, and led engineering of the matching and market-data platform at Sphinx (SPHX). Earlier, I built William Hill's Product Data Service, the system behind every sports price change and bet, and Coull's OpenRTB bid switch for video advertising.

I work mostly in Go, Rust, and Python, on Kubernetes across AWS and Google Cloud. I am listed among the [go-ethereum authors](https://github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum/blob/master/AUTHORS), have contributed to Gossamer, and have put RAG, LLM, and voice AI pipelines into production, including at Genie AI.

I stay close to production. Incident postmortems, on-call runbooks, monitoring, load tests, and staged rollouts are part of the job, not an afterthought.

You have a vision. I help turn it into a system that can actually run.
